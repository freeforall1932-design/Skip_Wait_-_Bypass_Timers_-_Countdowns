Free & Unlimited

No limits. No key. No account.

Countdown timers and waiting pages are skipped automatically.

  • Unlimited use
  • Clicks “Continue” when a real step needs it
  • Runs quietly in the background

Binds a domain to an existing skip engine without an update. Takes effect on new page loads; the site must use the chosen engine's layout.

Adds a “Try audio assist” button to the Skip Wait overlay when a page is stuck on a reCAPTCHA. Nothing runs on its own — the attempt happens only when you press that button. The audio clip is sent only to the speech-to-text server you configure below (e.g. your own Whisper server). Leave empty to disable; you always solve manually first.
